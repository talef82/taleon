HIGHLIGHTS

I'm Jon Flores, the mind and soul behind the Taleon project. Based in Spain, I've got serious skin in the game: I've been behind the decks as a DJ since 1996 and crafting tracks in the studio since 2002. My journey in the industry also includes founding my own label, Undercool Productions, back in 2009.

After years of musical evolution, I launched my first single under the Taleon alias in 2022, focusing my sound entirely on Organic, Progressive, and Melodic House.

My ultimate goal is simple: to share music that is undeniably danceable, yet deeply emotional. I want to transmit good vibes through melodies that make your mind travel, provoke thought, or simply become the perfect soundtrack accompanying you through life.

“Taleon emerged in 2022, but the definitive momentum is happening now. Time to share it with the world.”

25+ YEARS BEHIND THE DECKS: THE CRAFT, THE EVOLUTION & THE SOUND

At 14 years old, I bought my first records and stepped behind a pair of turntables. I never left the booth.

Over the past 25+ years as professional, I have lived every chapter of modern DJ culture—navigating the shift from pure vinyl to WAVs, MP3s, and CDJs without losing the raw tactile instinct of crowd control. Playing weekend after weekend across every conceivable venue and musical style has made reading a room second nature.

CROWD PSYCHOLOGY & EXTENDED JOURNEYS

Energy is everything. The dynamics change, but the mission remains identical:

  • Intimate Venues (200 capacity): Locking into close-quarters tension, nuance, and deep groove.
  • Large-Scale Clubs (500–1,000+ capacity): Driving heavy, room-filling momentum that commands the main room.

I specialize in extended sets of 5+ hours. Club music isn't meant to be rushed into a one-hour festival sprint; it is an evolving narrative designed to build tension, create hypnotic peaks, and take the dancefloor on a complete psychological journey.

100% ORIGINAL: THE ENGINE BEHIND THE RELEASES

My booth is an extension of my studio. Every DJ set I perform is 100% composed of my own productions, unreleased edits, and exclusive club tools.

The relentless pace of my catalog output isn't a marketing tactic—it is a functional necessity for performance. Building multi-hour journeys entirely from proprietary material demands a constant flow of fresh grooves and basslines, melodic hooks, and rhythmic tension built specifically to test and break on the dancefloor in real time.
